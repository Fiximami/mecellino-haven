## Safety, consent and who is responsible for your child

Programme safeguarding information for adults responsible for a young person. Youth Discovery Gateway sits under Capacity Building. This page describes rules the programme is designed to work to — not live reporting routes.

Recruitment and live application intake remain closed until programme and operational, privacy and insurance readiness gates are closed. Organisation safeguarding reporting contact details are not published until monitored channels are verified and approved.

Eligible ages are 13–25, calculated at the official cohort start date. Age is used only for eligibility and consent bands — not for aptitude scoring, ranking or automatic selection. Youth aged 10–12 are not currently eligible for Youth Discovery Gateway. They must not appear in active YDG recruitment, eligibility, consent or participant-facing programme bands.

## What YDG is

- A structured programme where your child tries things, reflects, and writes their own next-step plan.
- At least five working days per cycle, supervised throughout.
- The Foundation pilot is intended to be free to families, subject to confirmed sponsorship.
- Your child keeps what they produce.

## What YDG is not

- Not a job, internship or placement scheme.
- Not a replacement for school, counselling or medical assessment.
- Not a test with a pass mark, and not an aptitude score.
- Not a guarantee of anything — including a place on the next track.

## Supervision

Who is with your child, and how many of them.

The ratios below apply to approved Foundation pilot delivery (ages 14–15) and broader programme delivery once age-specific approvals are recorded.

- **1 adult : 8 young people** — The maximum in normal workshop sessions.
- **1 : 6 or stricter** — Practical demonstrations or an unfamiliar venue, after risk assessment.
- **1 : 4 or stricter** — Higher-risk activity, water, machinery, crowded events or transport transitions.
- At least two authorised adults are present for any activity with under-18s. Never one adult alone.
- Only adults who are trained, physically present and actively supervising are counted. Registration, photography, driving and admin do not count.
- A 30-participant day requires at least four active supervising adults, plus accessible safeguarding support.
- Every adult is screened and trained before working with young people.

### Ages 10–12

Youth aged 10–12 are not currently eligible for Youth Discovery Gateway. They must not appear in active YDG recruitment, eligibility, consent or participant-facing programme bands.

## Consent and your child's own agreement

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Taking part | Required |
| First aid and emergency care | Required |
| Supervised group trips | Required |
| Transport arrangement | Required |
| Code of conduct | Required |
| Photographs and video | Optional |

Saying no to photographs would not affect your child's place. Media permission is asked separately, on its own screen, and you can change your mind at any time.

For ages 13–17 we need your programme consent *and* your child's own assent, recorded separately. Your child can decline, and nothing bad happens if they do.

For participants aged 18–25, the young person gives their own legal consent. A parent, guardian or approved responsible adult still gives programme acknowledgement as an eligibility condition, but this never overrides the participant's own consent or their right to withdraw.

Where involving a parent would be impossible, unsafe or inappropriate, the Safeguarding Lead may approve an alternative responsible adult through a documented exception process before participation, once the required safeguarding governance is in place.

No participant and approver may use the same identity or telephone number unless this is independently verified through an approved accessibility accommodation.

## Arrival and collection

- You arrange and pay for arrival and collection, unless we agree something else in writing.
- We record who is allowed to collect your child, and we check them at sign-out.
- We will not release your child to anyone who is not on that list.
- Any official Mecellino Haven transport is separately consented, risk-assessed, recorded and insured.

Late or failed collection: two adults keep supervising while we contact you and your emergency contacts.

## If a child is in immediate danger

Contact emergency services first. Do not wait for us. Organisation safeguarding reporting routes are not open in this milestone.

Safeguarding reporting contact details, including routes to raise concerns with the organisation, will be published only after the safeguarding manual is adopted, monitored channels are verified, and recruitment readiness gates are closed. Until then, this page provides programme information only.

- When reporting routes open, concerns will be recorded in your words.
- Secrecy cannot be promised. Information is shared only with people who can help keep someone safe.
- Allegations are referred to the appropriate authority — not investigated solely by programme staff.
- Nobody is punished for raising a concern.

## Questions parents ask

**Will families pay a fee?**

The Foundation pilot is intended to be free to families, subject to confirmed sponsorship.

**Will you photograph my child?**

Only if you give separate written permission, and only for the uses you tick. You can allow some uses and refuse others. Refusing entirely would not affect your child's place when intake opens.

**My child has a disability or finds reading hard. Can they still apply?**

Yes. Disability, limited literacy, no formal schooling or lack of interview polish is not an automatic exclusion. We consider reasonable accommodation first, and support needs never count against an applicant.

**Do we need to be in a particular school?**

No. Some places come through selected schools and some through a community route that is open to young people who are not in school at all.

**What happens if we are not selected?**

You would receive a written reason, and a route to ask for the decision to be reviewed by someone who was not involved in making it.

**Can we change our mind after agreeing?**

Yes, at any time, by any channel, without giving a reason. We stop, and there is no penalty of any kind.
